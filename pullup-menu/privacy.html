Pull Up Menu Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

Pull Up Menu ("the app") is built by an independent developer. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

What the app uses

Location. While the app is open on your phone or Android Auto screen, or while you have turned on phone drive mode, it reads your device's location. It uses your location only to tell when you have stopped at a drive-thru and to find which restaurant you are at.

To identify the restaurant, the app sends your current coordinates to the Google Places API, a service run by Google. Google's handling of that request is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Notifications. If you allow notifications, the app shows one when it finds the restaurant you are at. Notifications are created on your device.

What the app does not do

  • It does not store your location history, on your device or on any server.
  • It has no accounts and does not collect your name, email, contacts, or photos.
  • It does not show ads, use analytics, or track you across apps.
  • It does not sell or share your information with anyone besides the Google Places request described above.

Data security

All requests the app makes are sent over encrypted HTTPS connections.

Children

The app is intended for adults who drive and is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

You can stop all location use at any time by closing the app, turning off drive mode, or removing the app's location permission in your phone's settings. Uninstalling the app removes everything it saved on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new effective date.
